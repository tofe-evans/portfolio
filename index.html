Flagship Build

Geospatial AI Readiness Assessment & Insights Engine

Stack: Power Automate • Dynamic PDF • HubSpot • Salesforce

A self-service diagnostic and enablement framework that evaluates an organization across 7 core capability dimensions (Strategy, Governance, Technology, Data, Workforce, Service Delivery, and Investment). The flow ingests assessment data, computes normalized maturity tiers, and automatically compiles an 8-page executive insights report complete with a sequenced 30/90/180-day operational roadmap.

Pipeline Architecture
01 / Intake & Data Ingestion

Captures multi-dimensional criteria across 7 domains via structured self-service web forms.

02 / Expression Normalisation & Maturity Categorisation

Averages domain scores and categorises maturity curve: Foundational (1) → Developing (2) → Established (3) → Integrated (4) → Transformative (5).

03 / Dynamic Roadmap Formulation

Constructs sequenced action pathways: 0–30 Days (Mobilise), 31–90 Days (Build & Validate), and 91–180 Days (Embed & Scale).

04 / Dynamic PDF Generation & Dispatch

Populates HTML template with SVG visuals and domain recommendations; automatically renders and emails an 8-page PDF to client stakeholders in under 60 seconds.

05 / CRM Triage & Account Routing

Routes consultation requests via HubSpot into Salesforce queues, assigning actionable advisory leads directly to sales teams.
